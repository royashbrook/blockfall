## 2026-07-01 Performance Architecture Pass

Roy asked whether voxels/chunks were the wrong foundation and then approved a broad performance pass. Conclusion: keep voxels as the truth layer, but stop paying full near-field costs for every far/static/hidden thing.

Patch state after this pass:

- Profiling signal: added opt-in `BF_PERF_LOG=1` renderer logging with FPS, draw count, prop count, live/retired/reusable buffer counts, reusable MB, and new-vs-reused Metal chunk buffer counters.
- Upload discipline: `BufferRegistry` now recycles retired `MTLBuffer`s by power-of-two size bucket after the existing GPU-safety retirement delay, capped at 96 MB of reusable buffers.
- Frame-thread lighting: far never-meshed chunks can be uploaded first as unlit far-LOD meshes, then are marked dirty and promoted to fully lit meshes when the player comes within ~11 chunks.
- Draw-call pressure: far off-axis chunk culling is stricter than near culling, and the water translucency pass skips chunk meshes that contain no water instead of redrawing every terrain chunk and discarding in the fragment shader.
- Far terrain LOD / cheaper distant shading: far chunk draws carry an LOD bit through `bf_draw_item.material_id` into the Metal uniforms; the terrain shader skips procedural block detail, bump/specular work, and voxel sun-shadow raymarching for far chunks.
- Water metadata: chunk mesh records now cache whether the resident chunk contains water; `material_id` is used as a small render bitfield (`bit0 = far LOD`, `bit1 = has water`).
- Graphify was regenerated after this patch. Latest graph stats: `2,525` nodes, `6,369` edges, `153` communities.

Verification:

- `./ci/check.sh` green after the performance tranche.
